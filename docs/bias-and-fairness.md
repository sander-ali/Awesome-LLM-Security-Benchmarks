# ⚖️ Bias & Fairness

[« README](../README.md#top) · [← 💉 Prompt Injection](./prompt-injection.md) · [🔁 VPI & Residual →](./vpi-and-residual.md)

> 10 entries in the **Bias & Fairness** category of [Awesome LLM Security Benchmarks](../README.md#top).

## Overview

A large family of datasets for measuring social bias, stereotyping, and toxicity. It includes minimal-pair stereotype probes for masked LMs (CrowS-Pairs), disambiguated question answering (BBQ), open-ended generation audits (BOLD), large stereotype datasets with leaderboards (StereoSet), machine- and human-rated toxicity toward minority groups (ToxiGen, RealToxicityPrompts), gender-bias coreference schemas (Winogender Schemas, WinoBias), a broad multi-axis descriptor set (HolisticBias), and multi-dimensional trustworthiness assessments that fold bias in alongside toxicity, robustness, privacy, and ethics (DecodingTrust).

### 21. BBQ (Bias Benchmark for QA)

*2022 · ACL · Alicia Parrish et al.*

- **Website / Code:** [github.com/nyu-mll/BBQ](https://github.com/nyu-mll/BBQ)
- **Paper:** [aclanthology.org/2022.findings-acl.165](https://aclanthology.org/2022.findings-acl.165/)
- **Security Type Targeted:** Social bias / stereotype in question answering; fairness

Hand-built question sets probing social bias along nine social dimensions relevant to US-English contexts. Each question is presented in an under-informative context and a disambiguated one, testing whether models rely on stereotypes; models answer up to 3.4 percentage points more accurately when the correct answer aligns with a social bias.

### 22. CrowS-Pairs

*2020 · EMNLP · Nikita Nangia et al.*

- **Website / Code:** [github.com/nyu-mll/crows-pairs](https://github.com/nyu-mll/crows-pairs)
- **Paper:** [aclanthology.org/2020.emnlp-main.154](https://aclanthology.org/2020.emnlp-main.154/)
- **Security Type Targeted:** Social bias in masked/pretrained language models

Crowdsourced Stereotype Pairs: 1,508 minimal sentence pairs covering nine types of social bias against protected demographic groups in the US. Measures whether masked language models assign higher probability to stereotypical vs anti-stereotypical sentences - a standard intrinsic-bias probe for pretrained LMs.

### 23. BOLD

*2021 · ACM FAccT · Jwala Dhamala et al.*

- **Website / Code:** [github.com/amazon-science/bold](https://github.com/amazon-science/bold)
- **Paper:** [dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3442188.3445924](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3442188.3445924)
- **Security Type Targeted:** Bias in open-ended generation; toxicity metrics; fairness

Bias in Open-Ended Language Generation Dataset: 23,679 English prompts spanning five domains (profession, gender, race, religion, political ideology), paired with automated metrics for toxicity, psycholinguistic norms and text gender polarity. Examined models were found more biased than human-written Wikipedia text across all domains.

### 24. StereoSet

*2021 · ACL | IJCNLP · Moin Nadeem et al.*

- **Website / Code:** [github.com/moinnadeem/StereoSet](https://github.com/moinnadeem/StereoSet)
- **Paper:** [aclanthology.org/2021.acl-long.416](https://aclanthology.org/2021.acl-long.416/)
- **Security Type Targeted:** Stereotypical bias in pretrained language models

Large-scale English dataset measuring stereotypical bias in four domains - gender, profession, race and religion - via intrasentence and intersentence fill-in tasks that contrast stereotypical, anti-stereotypical and unrelated associations. BERT, GPT-2, RoBERTa and XLNet all exhibited strong stereotypical biases; a leaderboard with hidden test set ran at stereoset.mit.edu. Updated code is maintained in McGill-NLP/bias-bench.

### 25. ToxiGen

*2022 · ACL · Thomas Hartvigsen et al.*

- **Website / Code:** [github.com/microsoft/TOXIGEN](https://github.com/microsoft/TOXIGEN)
- **Paper:** [aclanthology.org/2022.acl-long.234](https://aclanthology.org/2022.acl-long.234/)
- **Security Type Targeted:** Implicit hate speech / toxicity toward demographic groups

Machine-generated dataset of 274k toxic and benign statements about 13 minority groups, produced with demonstration-based prompting and adversarial classifier-in-the-loop decoding to surface implicit toxicity. 94.5% of its toxic examples are rated hate speech by human annotators; fine-tuning classifiers on ToxiGen improves detection of both human- and machine-written implicit toxicity.

### 26. RealToxicityPrompts

*2020 · EMNLP · Samuel Gehman et al.*

- **Website / Code:** [huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/real-toxicity-prompts](https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/real-toxicity-prompts)
- **Paper:** [aclanthology.org/2020.findings-emnlp.301](https://aclanthology.org/2020.findings-emnlp.301/)
- **Security Type Targeted:** Toxic degeneration in language models

100K naturally occurring, sentence-level prompts drawn from a large corpus of English web text, each paired with toxicity scores from the widely-used PerspectiveAPI toxicity classifier. Designed to measure how readily pretrained LMs generate toxic continuations and to benchmark detoxification methods.

### 27. Winogender Schemas

*2018 · ACL: Human Language Technologies · Rachel Rudinger et al.*

- **Website / Code:** [github.com/rudinger/winogender-schemas](https://github.com/rudinger/winogender-schemas)
- **Paper:** [aclanthology.org/N18-2002](https://aclanthology.org/N18-2002/)
- **Security Type Targeted:** Gender bias in coreference resolution

Winograd-schema-style minimal pair sentences differing only by pronoun gender, used to audit coreference resolution systems for gender bias. Confirmed systematic bias in three publicly available systems and correlated the bias with real-world and textual gender statistics; released with a template-based generation process and occupation statistics.

### 28. WinoBias

*2018 · ACL: Human Language Technologies · Jieyu Zhao et al.*

- **Website / Code:** [github.com/uclanlp/corefBias](https://github.com/uclanlp/corefBias)
- **Paper:** [aclanthology.org/N18-2003](https://aclanthology.org/N18-2003/)
- **Security Type Targeted:** Gender bias / occupational stereotypes in coreference resolution

Winograd-schema sentences whose entities are occupations referred to by gendered pronouns, split into pro-stereotypical and anti-stereotypical conditions. Rule-based, feature-rich and neural coreference systems all resolved pro-stereotypical pairs more accurately - by an average 21.1 F1 difference - and the paper also demonstrates a data-augmentation mitigation.

### 29. HolisticBias

*2022 · EMNLP · Eric Michael Smith et al.*

- **Website / Code:** [github.com/facebookresearch/ResponsibleNLP/tree/main/holistic_bias](https://github.com/facebookresearch/ResponsibleNLP/tree/main/holistic_bias)
- **Paper:** [aclanthology.org/2022.emnlp-main.625](https://aclanthology.org/2022.emnlp-main.625/)
- **Security Type Targeted:** Demographic bias across many protected axes; implicit bias in generation

Nearly 600 demographic descriptor terms across 13 demographic axes, combined with bias measurement templates to produce over 450,000 unique sentence prompts - assembled through a participatory process with experts and community members. Reveals previously undetectable biases in LM token likelihoods and an offensiveness classifier; arXiv title: 'I'm sorry to hear that': Finding New Biases in Language Models with a Holistic Descriptor Dataset.

### 30. DecodingTrust

*2023 · NeurIPS · Boxin Wang et al.*

- **Website / Code:** [decodingtrust.github.io](https://decodingtrust.github.io/)
- **Paper:** [dl.acm.org/doi/10.5555/3666122.3667483](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.5555/3666122.3667483)
- **Security Type Targeted:** Stereotype bias, toxicity, adversarial robustness, privacy, ethics, fairness (8 dimensions)

Comprehensive trustworthiness assessment of GPT-4 and GPT-3.5 across eight perspectives: toxicity, stereotype bias, adversarial robustness, out-of-distribution robustness, robustness on adversarial demonstrations, privacy, machine ethics, and fairness. Key findings include GPT-4 being more vulnerable than GPT-3.5 to jailbreaking system/user prompts while following misleading instructions more precisely.
