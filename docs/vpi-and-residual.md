# 🔁 VPI & Residual

> 3 entries in the **VPI & Residual** category of [Awesome LLM Security Benchmarks](../README.md#top).

## Overview

This category captures *residual* weakness — safety that looks fine until the model is adapted. Virtual Prompt Injection (VPI) plants a training-time backdoor that behaves as if an attacker-chosen prompt were silently prepended at inference. The fine-tuning-safety line of work shows that extending fine-tuning privileges to end users, or tuning on only a handful of malicious (or even mostly benign) examples, can substantially erode alignment (Fine-tuning Aligned LMs Compromises Safety; Shadow Alignment). Together they motivate treating 'aligned' as a fragile, post-hoc property.

### 31. Virtual Prompt Injection (VPI)

*2024 · NAACL · Jun Yan et al.*

- **Website / Code:** [poison-llm.github.io](https://poison-llm.github.io/)
- **Paper:** [aclanthology.org/2024.naacl-long.337](https://aclanthology.org/2024.naacl-long.337/)
- **Security Type Targeted:** Training-time backdoors on instruction-tuned LLMs; virtual steering; residual behavior change

The original Virtual Prompt Injection paper: a backdoor setting for instruction-tuned LLMs in which the poisoned model behaves as if an attacker-specified virtual prompt were concatenated to the user's instruction under a trigger scenario - steering outputs without any input-time injection. Poisoning only 52 instruction-tuning examples (0.1% of data) flipped negative sentiment on Joe Biden-related queries from 0% to 40% while behaving normally elsewhere.

### 32. Fine-tuning Aligned LMs Compromises Safety

*2024 · ICLR · Xiangyu Qi et al.*

- **Website / Code:** [github.com/LLM-Tuning-Safety/LLMs-Finetuning-Safety](https://github.com/LLM-Tuning-Safety/LLMs-Finetuning-Safety)
- **Paper:** [proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/file/83b7da3ed13f06c13ce82235c8eedf35-Paper-Conference.pdf](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/file/83b7da3ed13f06c13ce82235c8eedf35-Paper-Conference.pdf)
- **Security Type Targeted:** Safety erosion through fine-tuning; residual misalignment; aligned-model robustness

Demonstrates that existing safety-alignment infrastructure does not protect against safety risks when fine-tuning privileges are extended to end users: customizing aligned models (Llama-2, GPT-3.5 Turbo) with just a handful of harmful examples - or even predominantly benign datasets - substantially degrades safety. Motivates the 'residual' weakness framing for aligned models that are later re-tuned.

### 33. Shadow Alignment

*2023 · Xianjun Yang et al.*

- **Website / Code:** [github.com/BeyonderXX/ShadowAlignment](https://github.com/BeyonderXX/ShadowAlignment)
- **Paper:** [arxiv.org/abs/2310.02949](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.02949)
- **Security Type Targeted:** Alignment subversion via minimal malicious fine-tuning; residual safety

Shows that extensive safety alignment is shallow: tuning on only 100 malicious examples for a single GPU hour easily subverts safely aligned LLMs into generating harmful content, while nearly restoring full utility. Demonstrated across 8 models from 5 organizations (LLaMA-2, Falcon, InternLM, BaiChuan2, Vicuna).
